Sovereignty

Why the Gateway Makes Sovereignty Stick

Models never execute tools directly. Every action against your systems runs through a Tool Hub inside your boundary — the model only ever sees a sanitized result back.

Routing by Residency Attribute

Every model deployment carries a residency attribute. Confidential data is routed only to EU-resident providers under Article 28 data processing agreements with zero-retention defaults — no training on your prompts, with routing designed to minimize exposure to extraterritorial data-access requests.

One Policy Decision Point

One policy decision point evaluates every model call and tool call. Guardrails check input and output, classify the request against Public / Internal / Confidential / Restricted tiers and route sensitive data to the right model and approval path.

What the Gateway Controls

  • Virtual keys bound to IdP identity, not shared accounts
  • Guardrails and content policy at the edge
  • Model routing by data tier and residency
  • Usage budgets and cost attribution per team

What the Tool Hub Controls

  • Per-tool and per-user authorization
  • Credential brokering without exposure
  • Approval gates for production writes
  • Execution stays inside your boundary
