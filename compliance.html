Controls That Cover Multiple Frameworks

Instead of building a separate evidence packet for each auditor, we design the platform so the same artifacts satisfy overlapping requirements.

01

Inventory & Classification

A system inventory with risk classification feeds ISO 42001, EU AI Act, NIST CSF and GDPR records of processing in one pass.

02

Access & Identity

Logging & Oversight

EU AI Act

Core Obligations Since August 2026

The EU AI Act is not a future problem. The controls below are built into the platform from the first sprint.

System Inventory & Risk Classification

We maintain an inventory of every AI system, classify risk, and document intended purpose, data sources and affected users. That inventory feeds both EU AI Act and ISO 42001 registers.

Logging & Human Oversight

Every prompt, model call, tool call and approval is logged with attribution. Human oversight is enforced at the gateway for restricted actions and high-risk outputs — not as an afterthought.

EU AI Act Checklist

  • System inventory with risk tier
  • Technical documentation per system
  • Logging of operation and outputs
  • Human oversight for high-risk cases
  • Accuracy, robustness and security testing

  • AI system impact evaluation
